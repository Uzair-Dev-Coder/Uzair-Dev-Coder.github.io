      Welcome to Chai Jan

      At Chai Jan, we bring people together over the perfect cup of tea. Our vibrant café is designed for those who appreciate authentic flavors and a cozy, aesthetic atmosphere perfect for late-night conversations and creating lasting memories with friends and family. More than just a tea spot, we take pride in serving high-quality quick bites, from crispy parathas to flavorful chaats, ensuring every visit satisfies your cravings. Come for the exceptional chai, stay for the welcoming vibe, and experience the heart of our community.

      • Tea, coffee, quick bites, small plates, chaat and parathas
      • Casual, cozy seating for groups and families
      • Table service
      • Free parking lot and free street parking
